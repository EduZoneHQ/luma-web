Launch Checklist

Built for more than pretty screenshots.

Every launch should cover the technical basics too.

🔎 Search

Title, description, headings, local keywords, sitemap and indexing setup.

📱 Mobile

Responsive layouts, touch-friendly controls, fast loading and clear CTAs.

📊 Analytics

Traffic measurement, conversion events and basic performance review.

🔐 Security

SSL, secure hosting configuration and sensible access management.

💬 Conversion

WhatsApp, calls, forms, maps and enquiry paths where relevant.

🚀 Handover

Domain, hosting, admin access, documentation and post-launch support.

Business Essentials

Clear terms build trust.

Starter policy text for review and customisation before publishing as legal documents.

Privacy

Luma Web should collect only information needed to provide requested services, communicate with clients and manage projects. Personal information should not be sold. A final policy should identify the actual data collected, processors, retention periods and contact method.

Terms of Service

Projects should define scope, deliverables, revision limits, client responsibilities, timelines, third-party costs, hosting/domain ownership and acceptance. Final legal terms should be reviewed for the jurisdiction and business model.

Payment & Refunds

Before accepting payments, clearly state deposit requirements, when work begins, what is refundable, how cancellations are handled and how outstanding balances affect launch or handover.

Maintenance

Monthly care should specify included update volume, response expectations, third-party subscriptions, excluded work and cancellation terms.
